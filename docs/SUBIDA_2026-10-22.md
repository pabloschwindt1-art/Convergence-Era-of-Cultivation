## 1. Core 0.1.3
```
0.1.3
- Wuxia work rooms: alchemy room, spirit smithy, scribe hall and weaving room. Cleanliness, impressiveness, qi and linked furniture raise the quality of what is made there; a good room makes up for a weak artisan.
- Alchemy can fail: about 30% bad or failed (waste pills), 45% normal, 20% excellent, 5% celestial for an average alchemist; masters, good rooms, a better furnace fire and alchemist traits push the odds up. New colonist stat: alchemy mastery.
- Pills, weapons and talismans show who made them: your colonist, or a lore maker for items from outside. Pills show their real effect for their quality, side effects and what the recipe needs.
- Lore pass: descriptions now name the five sects of Convergence: Factions; the Ancient Dragon Clan is a vanished legend.
```

## 2. Alchemy & Artifacts 0.1.1
```
0.1.1
- The alchemy furnace needs fire: wood, spirit stones or fire crystals, and a better fire makes better pills.
- Alchemy can fail: a failed refinement leaves waste pills. Average alchemist: about 30% bad or failed, 45% normal, 20% excellent, 5% celestial.
- The furnace, spirit forge, rune table and talisman table form their own rooms (alchemy room, spirit smithy, scribe hall): clean, impressive rooms full of qi with the right furniture make better work.
- Lore pass: descriptions now name the five sects of Convergence: Factions; the Ancient Dragon Clan is a vanished legend.
- Now compatible with RimWorld 1.7 (Waylith engine). Still works on 1.6: same files for both versions.
```
