# Convergence: Era of Cultivation

*A fragment of the Nine Heavens collides with your rimworld. Qi begins to flow, and the Era of Cultivation begins.*

Convergence is a modular xianxia / wuxia cultivation system for RimWorld 1.6, inspired by Tale of Immortal and cultivation fantasy.

**Build your own story.** Become a spiritual cultivator, master body cultivation, combine technology with the Dao, or establish a traditional sect.

Convergence expands RimWorld's existing mechanics rather than replacing them.

## Core Features

- **The Convergence:** Choose when spiritual energy awakens. The world changes as spirit roots, factions, beasts and resources become active.
- **Spirit Roots:** Six elements and four grades of spiritual potential, integrated with Biotech genes when available.
- **Cultivation Realms:** Nine realms across two paths: Qi Cultivation and Body Cultivation.
- **Heavenly Tribulations:** Break through cultivation realms while facing lightning, spiritual trials and the risk of death.
- **Martial Techniques:** 44 techniques across nine manuals, featuring mastery and combat combinations.
- **The Master:** A mysterious wounded cultivator arrives to guide your colony and introduce the cultivation system through events.
- **Qi Networks:** Spiritual conduits and consumers provide an alternative to electricity.
- **Celestial Calendar:** Qi Tides, Qi Ebbs and the Moon of the Inner Demon.
- **Celestial Dissonance:** Implants affect the difficulty of heavenly tribulations.
- **Dao Research:** A dedicated cultivation research system.
- **Primordial Crystal:** Ten spirit stones pressed into a stable crystal at the stonecutter's table. A shared spiritual material used by the modules (first used by the Qi-Tech spirit resonator).
- **Stories and Characters:** Cultivation traits, extensive backstories and character progression.

## Your World, Your Story

Cultivation is not mandatory for every colonist.

Spiritual warriors, technological soldiers, mechanitors and ordinary colonists can coexist. Traditional cultivation and advanced technology can be combined, with consequences for progression and tribulations.

The original RimWorld mechanics remain available.

## Customization

- Choose when the Convergence begins.
- Configure spirit-root probability.
- Select cultivation difficulty: Easy, Hard or Nightmare.
- Enable or disable lethal tribulations.
- Configure the Master and celestial dissonance.
- Play with or without industrial technology.

## Modular Addons

Convergence consists of one Core framework and 14 optional modules:

Resources, Formations, Races, Traits & Destinies, Sects, Alchemy & Artifacts, Apparel, Fauna & Flora, Furniture, Grafts, Stories & Scenarios, Qi-Tech, Biotech Wuxia and Flying Sect.

Each module expands the world with new mechanics, content and storytelling possibilities.

## Compatibility & Requirements

**Requires:** RimWorld 1.6 and Harmony.

Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey are optional for the Core. DLC-specific features activate when their expansions are installed.

No electricity or industrial research is required.

## A Personal Project

I'm not a professional game developer. Convergence started as a personal hobby because I wanted to play a cultivation experience in RimWorld that I couldn't quite find in existing mods.

I originally made it for myself and decided to share it in case other players enjoy the same kind of experience.

This project was developed in my free time with the assistance of AI tools. I'll keep fixing bugs, improving mechanics and adding new ideas whenever I feel like working on it.

There is no development schedule or commitment to regular updates. This is simply a hobby that I enjoy, and I'm happy to share the results with anyone interested.

Feedback, suggestions and contributions are always welcome.
