# Convergence: Qi-Tech

Where ancient cultivation meets advanced technology.

Qi-Tech expands Convergence: Era of Cultivation with the scientific road to qi: research, machinery, weapons and defenses powered by qi, built by engineers who never meditated a day in their lives.

Cultivators understand qi through the Dao. The engineers of the Edge want to measure it, contain it and put it to work. Both roads are independent, and both can live in the same colony.

## Features

**Spiritual Engineering**

A new research tab for the scientific study of qi. It is researched at the hi-tech research bench like any other technology: no cultivators, spirit roots or epiphanies needed. Every project stays locked until the Convergence awakens the qi of the world.

Qi Fundamentals, Spirit Resonance and Advanced Spirit Instrumentation open the way to every Qi-Tech technology.

**Spirit Resonator**

The multi-analyzer of qi: a research facility for the hi-tech bench, built with primordial crystals and powered by the grid. It does not make or store qi; it lets your scientists measure it.

**Qi Weapons**

Guns that wound cultivators. Ordinary weapons barely scratch a high-realm cultivator; Qi-Tech weapons carry a spirit grade that cuts through their realm suppression.

- Qi induction rifle (grade 1)
- Concentrated qi rifle (grade 2)
- Qi compression cannon (grade 3: strikes a Golden Core cultivator as if they were mortal)

Any colonist can make and use them.

**Qi Turrets**

- Qi induction turret (grade 2)
- Qi containment cannon (grade 3, long range)

They need grid power and burn spirit stones with every shot. No power, switched off or out of stones: they stay silent.

**Qi-Powered Infrastructure**

- Qi converter: joins the qi network with the vanilla power grid (qi to electricity or electricity to qi).
- Qi-Tech exoskeleton: armor with a qi core and high celestial dissonance.
- Stabilization chamber: lowers the celestial dissonance of mechanical implants for a week.

## Integration with RimWorld

Qi-Tech is built on RimWorld's own research, power, crafting and turret systems: the Fabrication bench, the hi-tech research bench, facilities, refuelable turrets.

Qi-Tech weapons do not appear in raids or trader stock, so they do not change the starting difficulty.

It also integrates with Convergence: Formations through the qi network.
